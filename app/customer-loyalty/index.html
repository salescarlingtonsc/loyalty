What is Peekaa?

Peekaa helps businesses build and run customer loyalty programmes without relying on vanity points alone. Customers can receive real, spendable in-store credit and relevant offers, while businesses get a clearer view of repeat visits and customer relationships.

Customer loyalty

Create a loyalty programme that gives customers a reason to return and keeps the relationship active between visits.

Rewards & store credit

Reward customers with spendable value and offers that can encourage another visit.

Retention & engagement

Use customer activity, promotions and engagement tools to bring customers back more consistently.

Built for everyday businesses

Peekaa is designed for businesses such as F&B, salons, facial and beauty businesses, massage, fitness, retail and other customer-facing SMEs.
