How Peekaa works

Five things that turn a first visit into a habit.

Getting someone through the door once is marketing. Getting them back is a system. Peekaa is that system — sign-up, reward, message, insight and win-back, joined up in one place.

01Bring customers in

Signing up takes one scan.

Put your Peekaa QR code on the counter. A customer scans it, enters a name and a Singapore mobile number, and they are a member — before their coffee is ready.

  • No app download and no plastic card — it opens in their phone browser.
  • Marketing consent is asked for separately, never pre-ticked, and recorded against the customer.
  • Already have a customer list? Import it and carry on from there.

02Give them a reason to return

Points, stamps and tiers — your rules.

Choose how people earn: points on spend, a stamp card, or membership tiers that unlock better treatment as they keep coming back. Points and tiers can run side by side, so a regular is climbing towards status while still saving for the next reward.

  • You define the rewards and what they cost in points.
  • Redemption happens in front of you at the counter, not on trust.
  • Points and tiers stay independent — spending points never lowers a tier.

03Stay connected

Put your offer where your members already look.

Write one factual offer, add a photo, publish it. It becomes a live promotion inside the programme your members already opened to check their points — and they can pass it on to a friend on WhatsApp in a tap.

  • Publishing a promotion is a deliberate, permissioned act — not something any staff login can do.
  • Customers share to WhatsApp, Telegram or Facebook, or copy the link.
  • Each customer sets what they want to hear about, and can switch any of it off themselves.

05Bring inactive customers back

Win back the ones who stopped coming.

Peekaa knows who has gone quiet, because it knows when everyone last visited. Set a bring-back rule, and the reward is sitting in their programme waiting the next time they open it.

  • You choose how long counts as “gone quiet” and what the reward is.
  • See who has been away 30, 60 or 90+ days, as a list you can work through.
  • The rule measures return visits, so you see who actually came back.

The other side of the counter

A better experience for your customers, too.

A loyalty programme people quietly resent does not make regulars. Peekaa is built to be pleasant on the customer's side — which is exactly why it keeps working on yours.

  • Nothing to carry, nothing to lose

    Their points, tier and rewards live on their phone. No card to forget at home, no card to re-issue when it goes missing.

  • They always know where they stand

    The balance, the next reward and how far away it is are the first things they see. Progress they can see is progress they come back for.

  • They stay in control of the messages

    Consent is asked for once, plainly, and never pre-ticked. If they want fewer messages, they turn a category off themselves — they never have to ask you to do it. Receipts, booking confirmations and security messages are never affected.

  • One place for every programme they've joined

    Your programme sits alongside the others they've joined, so checking their points with you is a habit they already have.

What's inside

The rest of the day-to-day, in the same place.

Loyalty is the point of Peekaa, but a loyalty programme that does not know about your bookings and sales is guessing. So those live here too.

Bookings & appointments

Take bookings, manage a calendar per staff member, and let a completed appointment feed the loyalty programme by itself.

Record a sale

Ring up products or services from a tile catalogue, look the customer up by mobile number, and their points are earned on the spot.

Referrals

Every customer gets a code. Enter it under “Referred by” when their friend joins, and the referrer is rewarded once that friend actually spends.

Gift cards

Sell and redeem gift cards, with the money treated as cash collected rather than counted twice as revenue.

Memberships

Recurring plans with daily renewals and automated credit drops. Billing is collect-in-person for now — a renewal books the charge as a sale.

Prepaid packages

Sell a block of sessions up front and count them down as the customer uses them.

More than one branch

Run several outlets under one company. Your customers see one business; you see which branch the visit came from.

Roles & permissions

Give the counter staff what they need for their shift, and keep the money screens for the people who own them.

Business insights

Revenue, bookings, your busiest days, retention and team performance — read from what actually happened, not typed into a spreadsheet.

Your team's language

The business workspace runs in English, 中文 or Bahasa Melayu, chosen per person — so the counter staff read it in theirs. The customer rewards app follows each member’s choice of English, 中文, Bahasa Melayu or தமிழ்.
