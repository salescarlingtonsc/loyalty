## Scope

Seeds the v682 golden-business shape (`app.seed_golden_business_v682`) for 3 sectors
(fnb, salon, retail), then for each business reads FIVE independent revenue figures over
the same window and reconciles all four against the first:

* **A — recorded revenue.** `public.get_revenue_truth_v106` `totals.known_revenue_minor`.
* **B — dashboard summary.** `public.get_dashboard_summary_v155` `kpis.revenue_cents`.
* **C — AI sales window.** `app.v176_sales_window` `net_revenue_cents`.
* **D — platform consultant report.** `public.platform_get_assigned_firm_report_v94`
  `kpis.net_revenue_cents`, called as a real Google-SSO super admin.
* **E — direct SQL.** `sum(sales.amount_cents)` over the same qualifying-sale predicate,
  hand-written, no RPC.

Reconciliation percentage = `round(100.0 * X / A, 1)` for X in {B, C, D, E}; the fixture
asserts every one reads exactly `100.0`. As of nestly_v734/v737/v740/v742 (2026-09-02),
every reader in this estate excludes an `is_synthetic` client's sales from reported
revenue the same way `get_revenue_truth_v106` does, so all five figures reconcile
exactly for every seeded business — there is no known gap left for this fixture to pin.
